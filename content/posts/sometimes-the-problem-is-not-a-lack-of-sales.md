Sometimes, the problem is not a lack of sales.

Sometimes, the problem is **what happens after the sale.**

More customers can mean:

More orders.
More pressure.
More mistakes.
More delays.
More operational problems.

Before asking:

**"How can I get more customers?"**

Ask:

**"Can my business handle more customers?"**

Growth should not only increase revenue.

It should also improve the way the business operates.
